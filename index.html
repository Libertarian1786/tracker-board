Counted from RevenueCat, App Store installs only. Organic = no Apple Ads attribution. Installs here are people who opened the app, so they can differ from the Apple Ads install count above.

"Tapped buy, didn't finish" = pressed a buy button (RevenueCat paywall since 1.8.5, one-tap cards since 1.8.6) but has no trial and no purchase. One definition, used by every section.
